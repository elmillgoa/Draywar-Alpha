# DRAYWAR ALPHA — Vision & Philosophical Shift

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Status:** Governing document for the prove-it phase

---

## The Shift

The original plan was written as a complete studio-style game broken into clean phases.

That approach is excellent engineering.  
It is also how many ambitious solo + agent projects die: too much surface area before the core fantasy is proven.

**New governing question for the Alpha:**

> What is the smallest playable thing that proves the jurisdictional identity + personal recovery fantasy is actually fun?

Everything else is secondary until that question has a clear, playable answer.

---

## What We Are Protecting

These are non-negotiable even in the smallest version:

1. **No global alignment.** Standing is per-Entity and only enforced where that Entity has reach.
2. **The status moment.** Entering a system (and station) tells you what you *are* here. This remains the signature beat.
3. **Personal relationships as the realistic recovery lever.** Deep negative standing is sticky. The main way out is through people.
4. **Architecture that can grow.** Data shapes, EventBus usage, and service boundaries are designed for the full system even while content is tiny.

---

## What Success Looks Like

The Alpha is successful when a player (or Elliot) can say:

- “I understand why I’m treated differently in different places.”
- “Getting hated by someone powerful actually mattered, and climbing out through a person felt earned.”
- “I want to see what happens if I push this further.”

It does **not** need to be a complete game.  
It needs to make the core fantasy legible and desirable.

---

## Relationship to the Full Game

The Alpha is not a disposable prototype.

It is the first vertical slice of the real game.  
Systems are built with the full Destination in mind so that later expansion is mostly content, tuning, and additional behaviors — not redesign.

The full game still contains:
- Larger Entity and People population
- True hierarchy between Entities
- Richer personal help methods
- Full combat interlock and Operation layer
- Economy pressure and debt transformation
- The Holding endgame
- The complete 8-system cluster

Those remain the destination.  
They are simply not required to answer the first question.
