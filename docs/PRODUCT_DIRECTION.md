## Fantasy bar

**Space Skyrim** under Freelancer flight + Honorverse-style institutions:

- Dense hand-built sector you can get lost in  
- Main **story campaign** with real weight  
- Freedom to ignore the spine and still have a life (trade, fight, standings, People)  
- Radiant / generated volume so the world never feels empty  
- Not an MMO; not pure Elite-scale procedural galaxy  

Destination filters still apply: mouse-aim freighter life, jurisdictional identity, no planetary walking, no multiplayer.

---

## Campaign → sandbox (LOCKED shape)

1. **Campaign** runs from career start through:
   - debts paid off (money pressure is real, not optional), **and**
   - buying your own **Holding** (asteroid / station / equivalent sovereign rock)
2. **Ignition / Holding purchase** is the campaign climax endpoint (same structural rhyme as Destination: annexed open → own ground).
3. **After that:** true **sandbox** on the same save — world keeps running; no content wall; no forced “game over.”

Playable Holding-as-faction empire management beyond ignition remains optional / later (Destination already deferred deep post-ignition).

## Pillars Steam 1.0 must have

Miss any and the product stays a demo.

1. **Living sector** — traffic with purpose, local state that changes, enforcement that grows, news/rumors  
2. **Activity density** — contracts + opportunistic space events + station depth + risk geography  
3. **Real economy simulator** — see below (first-class; was under-weighted)  
4. **Standing / People** — already law; needs full career surface (recovery chains, Traitor, customs, patrols)  
5. **Story campaign spine** — authored peaks through Holding  
6. **Progression layers** — Ship → Operation → Holding  
7. **Sandbox continue** — after Holding purchase  
8. **Production presence** — art, audio, UI readability at Steam glance test  

---

## Economy (explicit missing pillar — LOCKED intent)

Static price tables with a few modifiers are **not enough**.

A game like this needs a **real economic simulator** at freighter-captain scale:

| Requirement | Plain meaning |
|-------------|----------------|
| Regional scarcity / abundance | Same good cheap in one system, expensive in another — for *reasons* |
| Prices that move | Over time and from play (trade volume, war, disruption) |
| Production / consumption | Stations (or regions) make and use goods; stock can run thin |
| Goods that matter | Cargo is the blood of the sector, not menu filler |
| Player impact | You can dent a local market; you cannot casually break the whole map |
| Readable | Captain can learn routes and risk without a spreadsheet PhD |

**Not required for 1.0:** full Eve-style player-driven multi-agent market, or real-time global commodity exchange UI.

**Tone filter:** money is bills and margins, not loot showers. Scarcity creates work and danger, not busywork.

Design detail (production nodes, tick rate, player weight) belongs in the product plan — not invented mid-build.

## Map philosophy (still Destination-aligned)
