# S6 Ops playtest brief

**Gate:** Ops feel — does a small fleet feel like progression past solo courier?

## What to try (10–15 min)

1. **Dock at Alpha Port** with enough credits for a hire (hire costs 800; start
   wallet is 500 — sell cargo, turn in a job, or console `credits set 5000`).
2. **Standing gate:** default standing is Neutral. Raise Reach Authority to
   Friendly (≥20) before hire works (recovery job, legal trade grind, or
   console standing). If hire buttons stay disabled, that is the gate.
3. **Hire a hauler** from the **Operations** section. Confirm credits drop and
   the fleet line shows 1 ship + hourly upkeep.
4. **Set haul order** (hauler only) — origin is the dock; destination prefers
   another station in the same system (Alpha Yard). Wait or advance time so a
   haul leg resolves (4 game hours). Market stock at both ends should move;
   wallet net changes with buy/sell on the abstract ship.
5. **Warehouse:** buy a little ore/scrap into the hold, **Deposit** into the
   station warehouse, undock/redock or save/load, **Withdraw** back. Capacity
   is 40 volume per station.
6. **Hire a second ship** (escort). Confirm max 2 — third hire refuses.
7. **Fire** a ship — upkeep should drop. No standing hit for a normal fire.
8. **Upkeep always runs** (docked or free-fly). If you zero the wallet and let
   misses stack (3 cycles), charter breach hits standing on the hire controller
   and that ship is released.

## What “feels like progression” should mean

- You can earn while not personally flying every leg (abstract haul).
- Money sinks (hire + retainer) and standing matter for growth.
- Warehouse lets you stage cargo without living in the hold.
- Escort is a **label only** this phase (no combat spawn) — still reads as
  “I run a small operation,” not a second player ship in space.

## Out of scope this gate

Campaign, Holding, empire sim, raising the 20-ship world budget, inventing
standing tiers.
