# Eras

Dated epochs. Open a new era when something foundational changes — engine
version, save schema, load-bearing architecture rewrite.

**Why:** a note from one era can lie in the next. Do not delete old records.
Date-fence them. Before trusting an old trap or journal note, check its era.

## When to open a new era

Engine upgrade, save schema major bump, or a rewrite that makes old traps unsafe
to apply blind. Journal an `AMENDMENT` and add a section above this line.
