### `NOTE` · 018#2 · 10:47 · Adversary pass caught two defects the green suite did not

1) Sell-then-buy at one dock was an unbounded money pump: +150 credits a cycle on Alpha Port medical, 15 markets exploitable, shelf back at opening stock every time. Cause: a trade is priced from the quote taken before it records its own shock, so a dump raises a glut and the buy-back is charged at the glutted price. The plan and economy_sim.md both wrote the invariant as 'buy-then-sell always loses' and the tests proved that sentence rather than the property. Fixed by capping shock magnitude against each commodity's own buy/sell spread, applied in the single writer and in the step size. 2) The naive production taper (1 - stock/capacity) silently throttled every producer to 57 percent of nameplate, turning 1.35x sector production into 0.77x and starving 25 of 64 markets to exactly zero over 10k steps, with no formula looking wrong. Fixed with a taper band. Lesson: test the invariant, not the sentence somebody wrote down.

### `NOTE` · 018#3 · 10:47 · S2 balance decisions worth not relitigating

Freight aims at each station's own target, not at equal relative stock: diffusion drags an importer up to its supplier's level, flattens the price gap and kills the route. Freight fills only to 45 percent of target so demand is never satisfied and the far spur always pays. Hop decay 0.85 over max 4 gates, chosen by sweep: 0.80 is the first value that clears the liveness band but leaves only 0.094 against a 0.08 floor; 0.85 gives 0.153 at a cost of under 1 credit per unit on the best routes. Seed factors 2.0 producer / 0.35 consumer are set by whole-credit rounding, not feel: at 1.8/0.45 grain and scrap both round to exactly zero margin at boot and the no-dead-commodities criterion fails. Seeds only move the opening state, so the settled equilibrium is identical either way (verified: thinnest 0.153 x target, fullest 0.872 x capacity at both). Black market deferred deliberately: it is a law change, not economy work.
