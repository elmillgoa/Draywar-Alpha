### `CONTRACT` · 032#2 · REPAIR-4 root cause: the cache had no way to refill

Attempt 2 was rejected for two measured regressions in the recovery Talk button, and they are one defect. The direct query was a **pull** — `StationMenu._refresh_recovery_buttons()` re-derived the offer person from the service on every refresh, so it was correct by construction. `on_recovery_offered` is a **push**, and `RecoveryService` emitted it from one place only: its own `_on_docked`. A push replaces a pull only if it fires on every transition that changes the answer. Five StationMenu handlers blanked the cache and nothing could refill it, so finishing a recovery step made the next step unreachable without undocking, and closing any Person hid an unrelated Person's Talk button.

Both attempts tried to fix this inside `StationMenu`, which cannot work — the information is not on the bus after the dock frame. The brief pointed them there: it said the cache *"must survive `_on_docked` and be cleared on undock and on the recovery-closed signals the menu already listens to"*, which is clear-on-close, refill-on-dock-only, and that **is** the defect. Attempt 2 implemented the brief exactly and was bounced for it.

Fixed on the emitter. `RecoveryService` keeps the berthed station, and re-announces on the four transitions that can change what `has_offer_for_person()` answers: dock; its own accept/complete/fail/abandon/betray; `on_person_closed`; `on_person_standing_changed`. Those four are everything `can_offer_recovery()` and `_next_step()` read — the person-standing writers in the whole repo are `CareerStart`, `RecoveryService` and the standing console, and all of them route through `set_person_standing`, which emits. The re-announce is **deferred**, never inline: that is what makes it order-independent against listeners blanking on the same signal, and against the writes a transition makes after its own emit (`record_personal_success`, `_mark_step_complete`, `clear_active`).

Also restored: three `StationDockQueries.offered_recovery_person` assertions attempt 2 deleted from `tests/test_e4_recovery_jax.gd`. They pass against the fixed source and they are the only coverage that function has — without them `DRAYWAR_CONVENTIONS.md` sanctions it as a "test helper only" that no test calls. The comment justifying the deletion cited `test_dock_offers_scoped_by_controller`, which does not exist; the real one is `test_dock_offer_jax_at_drift_not_at_reach`.
